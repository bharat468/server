## 3. Makan Malik (Landlord / Owner) Guide

Makan malik RentMate ko apne rozmara ke kamo ke liye step-by-step kaise use kare:

### Step 1: Login Kaise Kare? (Bina Password Ki Jhanjhat)
1. RentMate ki website par jayein aur **"Login"** par click karein.
2. Apna 10-digit mobile number dalein.
3. Aapke screen par ek **6-digit OTP** aayega, use daalkar verify karein.
4. Kisi password ko yaad rakhne ki zaroorat nahi hai.

---

### Step 2: Apni Building aur Flats (Units) Kaise Jodein?
1. Left menu me **"Properties"** par click karein.
2. **"Add Building"** button dabayein:
   - Building ka naam (e.g. *Krishna Residency*), pata, aur shehar dalein.
3. Building judne ke baad **"Manage Units"** par click karein:
   - Aap ek-ek karke flat (Flat 101, 102) daal sakte hain, YA
   - **"Bulk Generator"** se ek hi second me 1 se 4 floor ke saare flats auto-create kar sakte hain.

---

### Step 3: Kirayedaar (Tenant) Kaise Jodein aur Digital Lease Kaise Banayein?
1. Left menu me **"Tenants"** ya **"Applications"** par jayein.
2. Kirayedaar ka naam, mobile number, aur flat number chunein.
3. Monthly Rent (Kiraya) aur Security Deposit dalein.
4. **Late Fee Rule set karein**:
   - Kiraya mahine ki kis tarikh ko aana chahiye (e.g. *Har mahine ki 5 tarikh*).
   - Grace Period (chhoot ke din, jaise 3 din).
   - Agar 8 tarikh tak kiraya na aaye, toh kitna fine lagega (jaise ₹50 per day ya ₹500 fixed).
5. Jaise hi aap **"Activate Lease"** dabate hain:
   - Flat automatic **"OCCUPIED"** mark ho jata hai.
   - Ek legal digital agreement ban jata hai.

---

### Step 4: Bijli aur Paani ka Sub-Meter Bill Kaise Banayein?
1. Property units me flat ke saamne **"Meter Reading"** par click karein.
2. Is mahine ki current meter reading enter karein (e.g. Pichli reading 1200 thi, ab 1350 hui).
3. Software automatic **150 units** calculate karega aur organization ke fixed rate (jaise ₹9/unit) se multiply karke bill me add kar dega.
4. Galat reading (jo pichli se kam ho) system allow nahi karta, jisse galti ka koi chance nahi rehta.

---

### Step 5: Kiraya Recieve Karna aur Receipt (Parchi) Dena
1. **"Payments"** page par jayein.
2. Kirayedaar ne kiraya cash diya, GooglePay/PhonePe se diya ya bank transfer kiya — use select karke amount enter karein.
3. System turant ek **Official Rent Receipt (PDF)** generate karta hai jisme:
   - Flat number, kirayedaar ka naam
   - Base Rent + Bijli + Paani
   - Company/Building ka stamp aur signature space
4. Aap is PDF ko download karke WhatsApp par kirayedaar ko bhej sakte hain.

---

### Step 6: Maintenance (Kharabi) Ki Shikayatein Dekhna
1. Agar kirayedaar ne paani leakage ya fan kharab hone ki complaint dali hai, toh wo **"Maintenance"** page par dikhegi.
2. Aap plumber/electrician ko bhej kar ticket ka status **"In Progress"** se **"Resolved"** kar sakte hain aur kitna kharcha hua (Repair Cost) wo bhi record kar sakte hain.

## 4. Kirayedaar (Tenant) Guide

Kirayedaar ke liye RentMate ek transparent aur aasan anubhav deta hai:

1. **OTP Se Direct Login**:
   - Kirayedaar apne registered mobile number se login karke apna dashboard dekh sakta hai.
2. **Hisab Me Transparency**:
   - Kitna kiraya baaki hai, pichle mahine kitna diya tha, aur bijli ki kitni unit lagi thi — sab screen par saaf-saaf dikhta hai.
3. **Plumber / Bijli Complaint**:
   - Kisi bhi problem ke liye seedha "New Request" daal sakte hain taaki makan-malik use jaldi theek karwaye.
4. **HRA Tax Proof**:
   - Job karne wale kirayedaaron ko Income Tax bachane ke liye official rent receipt chahiye hoti hai, wo yahan se kabhi bhi download kar sakte hain.

```text
[1st Tarikh] ──────> System automatic Rahul ka Rent Invoice banata hai (₹12,000 Kiraya).
                      Rahul ke dashboard par notification dikhta hai.

[3rd Tarikh] ──────> Sharma Ji meter reading daalte hain: 100 Unit @ ₹9 = ₹900.
                      Total bill automatic update ho jata hai: ₹12,900.

[5th Tarikh] ──────> Due Date: Rahul online payment karta hai. 
                      Sharma Ji payment confirm karte hain aur 1 second me 
                      branded PDF receipt Rahul ko chali jaati hai.

[8th Tarikh tak] ──> Agar Rahul payment nahi karta, toh 3 din ke grace period ke baad
                      system automatic late fee fine (jaise ₹50/day) jodna shuru kar deta hai.
                      Makan malik ko call karke bolne ki sharmindagi nahi hoti!
```

## 9. Aam Sawal-Jawaab (Frequently Asked Questions)

### Q1: Kya mujhe computer aana zaroori hai?
> **Nahi.** RentMate ko itna aasaan banaya gaya hai ki koi bhi vyakti jisko smartphone chalana aata hai, wo 5 minute me ise seekh sakta hai.

### Q2: Agar kirayedaar ke paas smartphone na ho toh?
> Makan malik apne phone se hi unka hisaab maintain kar sakte hain aur unhe WhatsApp par receipt bhej sakte hain ya print de sakte hain.

### Q3: Bijli ki reading galat daal di toh?
> System pichli reading se kam reading daalne par turant rokk deta hai aur warning dikhata hai.

### Q4: Mera data safe hai?
> Haan. RentMate me bank-grade security aur daily automated backups hain. Aapka data 100% confidential aur passwordless OTP protected hai.
